## Chosen implementation

HAR Compression Audit targets JSON requests where the caller advertised compression and the capture recorded an identity response. It provides ranked routes, capture gaps and an optional byte budget with distinct results for known overage and insufficient evidence. The browser demo can process a local capture without a service. All bundled sample data is synthetic. HAR field semantics and Chromium’s explicit cache markers inform the classification rules. [HAR 1.2](https://github.com/ahmadnassri/har-spec/blob/master/versions/1.2.md) · [Chromium HAR exporter](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/devtools-frontend/blob/main/front_end/models/har/Log.ts) · [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#name-accept-encoding)
